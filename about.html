ZooSay™ About us

Here the zoo has its say.

Our mission

What we do

ZooSay pays UK adults fair, real cash for sharing their opinions, and gives businesses honest answers from real UK people at a clear, upfront price.

Our vision

Where we are going

A UK research panel where people are paid fairly for their time, businesses trust what they learn, and every member can move from the Nursery to the Safari.

Our goal

What we are working towards

To open to members and businesses on 31 January 2027, with real surveys and real money, and to launch publicly in the first quarter of 2027.

How we work: HEART

Five values guide everything we do, from how we pay members to how we look after our animals.

  1. H
    HonestWe say plainly what we pay, what we do and what we cannot promise.
  2. E
    EarnestWe mean what we say. Members are paid fairly and sincerely for real opinions.
  3. A
    Animal-kindOur animals are cared for and set free, and we treat members with the same care.
  4. R
    RespectfulWe respect every member's time, opinions and privacy.
  5. T
    TrustedUK only and real people, verified, so businesses and members can rely on us.

Why the zoo?

Our animals are in care for one reason: to save them, and the aim is always to set them free. Members follow the same journey. They start in the Nursery and move through the habitats as their approved surveys add up, with rewards along the way, until they are set free in the Safari.

Wildlife is the cause behind the story. We are building a way for members to give from their earnings to UK wildlife and conservation charities. No charity has been named yet, and we will say more once an agreement is signed.

ZooSay is UK only, for people aged 18 and over. We pay in pounds, not points.
