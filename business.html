Our rules

What we do and don't do

UK adults only

Every member is checked by UK mobile, UK postcode, UK connection and a bank account in their name.

No selfies or photos

We never ask members for photos, selfies, ID documents or biometrics.

Pounds, not points

Members are paid in real money. VAT at 20% is added to the full invoice.

You set the pace

Pause or cancel under clear rules. You approve each response.

Questions

Questions

Can I pause?

Yes. Pausing keeps your credits.

What if I cancel?

Before launch, everything comes back, VAT included, within 5 working days. While live, credits for responses nobody started come back within 5 working days. The rest is returned within 14 days of the last decision.

Can I get my balance back?

A credit balance is refunded on request.

Can you guarantee a number of responses or a date?

No. We can't guarantee response numbers or timings. We will tell you what we know, when we know it.

Who sees respondents' personal data?

Members' personal data stays with ZooSay. You only see the responses to your consumer audit.

Where can I ask a question?

Email help@zoosay.co.uk, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
