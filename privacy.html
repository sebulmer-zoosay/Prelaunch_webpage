1. Who we are

ZooSay™ is a trading name of SE BULMER LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14690326). Our registered office address is on our legal information page at zoosay.co.uk/legal. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller, registration number ZC268776.

If you have a question about your data, or want to use any of your rights, email help@zoosay.co.uk.

This notice covers ZooSay members, businesses that use ZooSay, and people on our waiting list. ZooSay is for people aged 18 or over who live in the UK.

2. What we collect

When you join as a member:

  • your email address, first name and surname;
  • your date of birth, to check you are 18 or over;
  • your UK mobile number, which we check with a text code;
  • your UK home address, which we check with a postcode lookup;
  • your internet connection (IP address and the country it comes from), to check you are in the UK and not using a VPN or proxy;
  • a record of when you accepted our terms and this notice, and which version.

While you use ZooSay:

  • "About you" answers you choose to give: gender, income band, the industry you work in and your interests. Each one is optional and you can say "I'd rather not say". We store your age group and region as bands, not your exact age or address;
  • which surveys you start, finish or are screened out of, how long they take, and your answers;
  • your earnings, balance, cashouts and the reasons for any rejection, appeal or hold;
  • the account you choose to be paid into: a UK bank account or a PayPal account. We keep the check result and a reference from the provider, not your full account details;
  • messages you send us and our replies;
  • a reason when you pause, cancel or leave, only if you choose to give one;
  • whether you want marketing emails, and your choices about them.

If you use ZooSay for a business: your name, work email, UK mobile number, the business's name, address and billing email, and records of what the business buys, its invoices and refunds.

If you join the waiting list: your email address and the first part of your postcode (for example DN2). Businesses can also give a company name, a contact name and a message.

If you visit our website: we count visits using Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and does not identify you. It records which page was viewed, the website you came from, your browser and type of device, and the country of your connection. Your IP address is used only to count the visit and is not stored.

What we never collect: selfies, photos, ID documents, fingerprints, face scans or any other biometric data. We do not ask for your National Insurance number.

4. How we keep your details apart from your answers

Your name, contact details, date of birth and address are kept in a separate, locked part of our database. Your survey answers are stored against a code, not your name. The part of ZooSay that handles surveys cannot read your personal details at all.

Businesses see your answers and general groupings such as "Age 25–34, North West". They never see your name, email, phone number or address, and they cannot link your answers across different surveys. The only exception is a survey that asks for your contact details, which you see on a separate screen naming the business and saying exactly what it wants and why. It is always your choice.

5. Your survey answers and the business that asked

When you answer a survey for a business, that business decides what to ask and how to use the answers, so it is the controller of your answers. We collect and store them for the business and follow its instructions, so for those answers we are its processor. Any question about how a business uses your answers can still come to us at help@zoosay.co.uk, and we will pass it on.

Comments you leave. If you choose to add a comment when you end a survey, ZooSay keeps it in the part of our database that holds personal details, and it is never shown with anything that identifies you. Businesses see only counts of common themes and how people felt, never your words. We remove personal details we can spot, and a comment that still looks personal is not shared. Please don't include names or contact details in a comment. Comment text is deleted 30 days after the survey closes. You can ask us to delete your comment at any time through help@zoosay.co.uk.

Surveys from our research partners. Some surveys may come from survey networks, or a business may use its own survey tool. When you open one of these you leave ZooSay and answer on their website. We send them only a code for that survey, never your name or contact details. Their own privacy notice covers what you tell them there.

GitHub and Cloudflare are based in the US, so some data leaves the UK. When it does, it is protected by UK adequacy rules (for the EU and the UK-US data bridge) or by the UK's approved contract terms (the International Data Transfer Addendum). Ask us at help@zoosay.co.uk if you would like more detail.

9. Automated checks and AI

We use automated checks, some of them AI, to score how carefully surveys are answered (for example speed, answer patterns and the quality of written answers) and to spot fake or repeated accounts. The score affects which surveys you are offered. If something looks wrong, a person looks at it before anything happens to your account or money: nothing is rejected or held automatically.

Otto, our help assistant, is an AI. Do not share passwords or bank details with Otto. Anything Otto cannot help with is passed to a person.

You can always ask for a person to review any decision about you, and you can object to this profiling, by emailing help@zoosay.co.uk.

10. Your rights

  • See your data: download a copy at any time from your account page, or ask us.
  • Correct it: ask for a correction from your account page. We reply within one month.
  • Delete it: close your account from your account page. Some records are kept for the times in section 8, for example because the law requires it.
  • Object to how we use your data, including the quality scoring, or ask us to restrict it.
  • Take your data elsewhere: your download is in a format other services can read.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or for sharing contact details with a business, at any time, in your account.

We reply to every request within one month. It is free. We may ask you to confirm who you are using the checks you did when you joined; we never ask for ID documents or photos.

11. Keeping your data safe

There are no passwords: you sign in with a link we email you, and we text a code to your mobile before a cashout or a change to where you are paid. Personal details are kept apart from survey answers, every part of the database has its own access rules, and every staff action is recorded. If something goes wrong with your data and it puts you at high risk, we will tell you without delay.

12. Complaints

Please tell us first at help@zoosay.co.uk, and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or phone 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this notice

If we make an important change we will email you and show it to you in the app at least 14 days before it starts. Each version is dated, and we keep a record of which version you accepted.
