Here the zoo has its say

Our animals are in care for one reason, to save them, and the aim is always to set them free. Members follow the same path: you start in the Nursery and move through the habitats as your approved surveys add up, with rewards along the way, until you are set free in the Safari.

  • UK only, for adults aged 18 or over.
  • Members are checked by UK mobile, address and bank account name. No photos, no selfies.
  • Wildlife is the cause behind the story. We are building a way for members to give from their earnings to UK wildlife and conservation charities.
