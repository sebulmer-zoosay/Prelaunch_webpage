How it works

For members

  1. Join the waiting list and we will email you when we open.
  2. Answer surveys about things you care about. You are paid 12p for every estimated minute.
  3. Cash out from £5, up to once a week, by PayPal or UK bank transfer, to an account in your own name.

For business

  1. Set up a consumer audit and buy Pre-funded Research Credits.
  2. Verified UK adults answer, and your credits pay for each response.
  3. Review each response. If you do nothing, it is approved after 14 days.

Questions

Who can join?

ZooSay is for UK residents aged 18 or over.

How much do I earn?

Members are paid 12p for every estimated minute of a survey. Each survey shows its reward before you start.

How do I get paid?

Once you have at least £5, you can cash out (up to once a week) by PayPal or UK bank transfer, to an account in your own name. Cash outs are limited to £800 in any rolling 12 months.

Do I need to send photos or ID?

No. We never ask for photos, selfies or ID documents. We check your UK mobile with a text code, your UK postcode, your connection and your bank account name.

Can I close my account?

Yes. Your whole balance is paid to you, with nothing taken off.

When does ZooSay open?

We are aiming to open in early 2027. Join the waiting list and we will email you the moment it opens.
